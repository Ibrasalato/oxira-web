Choosing a school management system is a decision you live with for years. It is where you will issue invoices, build timetables and talk to every family, and moving away from it later is costly for everyone.

This guide covers what a school system really needs to do, then gives you 10 questions to ask any vendor before you sign.

## What should a school management system cover?

Before comparing vendors, list everything your school runs today with separate tools. In most private schools the list includes:

- **Admissions**: an online application form, document upload and tracking through to enrollment.
- **Finance**: fees, installments and discounts, collections and reminders, and tax invoices.
- **Academics**: timetables and cover lessons, attendance, homework and exams.
- **Family communication**: a parent app instead of dozens of chat groups.
- **Leadership and quality**: tasks, approval paths, teacher evaluation and reports.

If a system covers only part of the list, you will keep running several systems with duplicated data that never quite matches.

## 10 questions to ask before you sign

### 1. Does it cover the whole school day in one platform?

Ask about admissions, finance, timetables, attendance and communication in one place. Every link between two separate systems is another weak point.

### 2. Are invoices compliant with ZATCA e-invoicing?

Schools registered for VAT in Saudi Arabia must issue e-invoices. Ask whether the system is integrated with the Fatoora platform for Phase 2, and whether it issues QR codes and credit notes. More in our article on [e-invoicing for private schools](/en/blog/zatca-e-invoicing-schools/).

### 3. Where is the data kept, and who can see it?

Student and family records are personal data protected by Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Ask about per-school data isolation, role permissions, two-step verification, the audit log and where it is hosted.

### 4. Are there parent and teacher apps?

The parent app is what families see every day. Make sure it is actually live on Google Play and the App Store, and try it yourself before you sign.

### 5. Is Arabic fully supported?

Not a partial translation, but a right-to-left Arabic interface, with Arabic reports and certificates, plus English for international schools.

### 6. How is my current data moved over?

Ask about importing from Excel or your current system, who does the migration, and how long setup and training take.

### 7. Does it work for a multi-branch group?

With more than one branch, you need a dashboard that brings performance, finance and alerts for every branch together, with central permissions.

### 8. Does it connect to the tools you use?

Such as Google Classroom and Microsoft Teams, payment gateways, Nafath digital identity and attendance devices.

### 9. Can it carry your school's name?

Some schools want the apps and platform under their own name, logo and domain. Ask whether that is possible and what it costs.

### 10. How is the price calculated?

Per student or per school, what each plan includes, and whether setup, data migration or training cost extra.

## Before you decide

Ask for a demo on data close to your school's reality, and a trial account your team can use for a week. The best test of a system is a real school day: lesson attendance, cover lessons and fee collection.
