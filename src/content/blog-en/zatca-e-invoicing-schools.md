Private schools issue hundreds of invoices every term: tuition, installments, transport, uniforms and activities. With e-invoicing from the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA), issuing invoices from a spreadsheet or an unconnected program is no longer acceptable.

This article explains what is required in plain terms. It is not tax advice; always check ZATCA's portal or your tax adviser for your school's situation.

## Two phases of e-invoicing

### Phase 1: generation

Phase 1 started on 4 December 2021. Since then, handwritten invoices or invoices edited in text programs are not allowed; invoices must be generated and stored electronically with the required fields, including a QR code on simplified tax invoices.

### Phase 2: integration

Phase 2 has been rolled out in waves since 2023. Invoicing systems must connect to ZATCA's Fatoora platform and issue invoices in a set format with additional fields. ZATCA notifies the businesses in each wave at least six months before their deadline.

For example, in July 2026 ZATCA announced the criteria for Wave 25: businesses with VAT-subject revenue above SAR 187,500 in any year from 2022 to 2025, with an integration deadline of 1 February 2027. Follow ZATCA's announcements to see which wave includes your school.

## What this means for your school

- **An invoicing system connected to ZATCA**: invoices must come from a compliant system connected to Fatoora, not from separate files.
- **Invoices linked to the student and parent account**: so installments, discounts and payments stay clear and match.
- **Credit notes**: refunds or fee changes need an electronic credit note instead of editing the original invoice.
- **Orderly archiving**: invoices must be stored and retrievable on request.

## Practical steps to prepare

1. **Know your position**: are you VAT-registered, and have you been notified of an integration wave?
2. **Review your current system**: does it issue compliant invoices, and does it connect to Fatoora for Phase 2?
3. **Bring fees and invoices together**: if fees live in one system and invoices in another, now is the time to unify them.
4. **Train the finance team**: on credit notes, discounts and installments inside the system.
5. **Test before the deadline**: don't wait for the last week; test the connection and sample invoices early.
